# V2 Source, Dataset & Asset Verification — Input Checklist

Use this checklist for the post-Batch-8 verification phase. Primary/official/academic material is preferred over secondary summaries.

For every item supplied from the internet, include where possible:

- source/dataset title;
- exact URL;
- publisher, institution, excavation project, museum, or repository;
- exact license/terms text;
- required attribution wording;
- redistribution and derivative-work permission;
- download/API URL;
- file format;
- coordinate reference system / datum where relevant;
- spatial resolution / scale;
- version/date;
- DOI/ISBN/pages for publications;
- the specific historical/archaeological claim it supports;
- uncertainties or limitations stated by the source.

## Priority A — terrain and basemap

- Copernicus DEM GLO-30 official documentation/license/download details.
- NASA SRTM official documentation/license/download details.
- Natural Earth current license/attribution details.
- PMTiles conversion/encoding documentation for the selected DEM pipeline.

## Priority B — Sinai / Exodus / Yam Suph

- academic paleohydrology and paleochannel studies for the eastern Nile Delta;
- ancient lake/marsh/coastline reconstructions relevant to Yam Suph proposal families;
- Sinai geology/topography and wilderness-environment sources;
- academic sources for Jebel Musa tradition, Har Karkom, and northwestern-Arabia proposal families, including objections.

## Priority C — Canaan / Megiddo / Jezreel

- official/current Megiddo excavation project sources;
- archaeological plans/GIS and reuse rights;
- Jezreel Valley landscape/environment studies;
- authoritative geographic data for Carmel, Tabor, Gilboa, Jordan Valley, Shephelah, Galilee, and Negev;
- Bronze/Iron Age road/corridor datasets with licensing.

## Priority D — Jerusalem / Moriah / Zion / Passion

- City of David / southeastern ridge archaeology;
- Gihon Spring, Hezekiah's Tunnel, Siloam and Pool of Siloam sources;
- Temple Mount physical/topographic data that does not overclaim First Temple footprint certainty;
- Kidron Valley and Mount of Olives terrain sources;
- first-century/Herodian Jerusalem wall/topography datasets;
- academic treatments of praetorium candidates;
- Golgotha and burial-site candidate scholarship;
- licensed archaeological plans/images for Holy Sepulchre / Garden Tomb contexts where available.

## Priority E — Galilee and Jesus ministry geography

- Sea of Galilee terrain/shoreline data;
- Capernaum, Magdala, Nazareth, Caesarea Philippi excavation/project sources;
- Bethsaida candidate scholarship and project data;
- Cana candidate scholarship;
- Judean wilderness environmental sources;
- baptism-location / Bethany-beyond-the-Jordan sources.

## Priority F — imperial / Pauline cities

- Babylon and Nineveh official/academic archaeological datasets/site plans;
- Susa and Persepolis archaeological/heritage datasets;
- Ephesus excavation data, especially harbor/shoreline change;
- Roman-road datasets for Puteoli → Appian corridor → Rome;
- first-century Rome topography datasets;
- Patmos historical topography and licensed imagery.

## Priority G — gazetteers and identifiers

- Pleiades current license/attribution/API/download details;
- relevant Pleiades IDs for flagship places;
- Wikidata license/API details and relevant QIDs;
- AWMC / DARE or other ancient-road datasets, exact license and chronology/coverage.

## Priority H — imagery / panoramas / museums

For every image or panorama:

- exact object/image page;
- creator/photographer/institution;
- copyright status;
- exact license;
- derivative permission;
- attribution statement;
- highest permitted resolution;
- whether hotlinking is allowed or local/CDN redistribution is required.

## Priority I — paleoenvironment

- pollen/paleobotanical datasets;
- Holocene vegetation/climate studies;
- Dead Sea level reconstructions;
- regional hydrology/coastline studies;
- stated chronology, spatial resolution, uncertainty, and reuse license.

## Release rule

A third-party source or asset should not be marked `primary-verified`, and a scene should not move from `prototype` to `ready`, merely because a citation or URL was supplied. The source must be checked for both **claim support** and **reuse/license compatibility**.
